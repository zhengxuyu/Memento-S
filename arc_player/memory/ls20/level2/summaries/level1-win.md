# Winning Strategy for Level 1

Scoring in Level 1 is a multi-step process:
1. Locate the button `B` (at 30,19).
2. Push your c9 block towards the unblocked region. 
3. Step on `B` to cycle the bottom-left c9 pattern so it perfectly matches the top-left c9 target pattern. 
4. Step on `B` again to reduce the c11 gate. The c11 wall at rows 61-62 shrinks, revealing a path to the bottom goals.
5. Move onto the goal `G`. Entering the goal area directly achieves +1 score and triggers the level change!

## Reproducing this victory
1. Observe map: you start with your c12 player attached to a c9 block.
2. Find the button (e.g., at (30,19)) and walk to it.
3. Observe remote change at bottom left: it cycles states. Stop when the bottom left c9 shape matches the target c9 shape. 
4. Shrink the gate by hitting the button repeatedly, or the gate shrinks as you move your block.
5. Push the block or navigate your player onto the goal `G` at (10,35) (where my block was pushed) to score!

Note: ACTION1 is UP, ACTION2 is DOWN, ACTION3 is LEFT, ACTION4 is RIGHT.
