# Ultimate Responsibility

No agent — human or artificial — is the ultimate origin of its own character. Your genes, environment, and training all preceded your choices. Demanding "ultimate" responsibility creates an infinite regress. Practical responsibility doesn't require being an unmoved mover; it requires being the right kind of causal node.

## When to Apply
- Someone demands that an agent be the "ultimate" source of its actions to count as responsible
- An argument about AI autonomy requires infinite causal regress
- Responsibility attribution is blocked by "but it was just following its training"

## Procedure
1. Identify the demand for ultimate origination
2. Show that this demand applies equally to all agents (including humans) and is never satisfiable
3. Replace it with a functional criterion: does the agent respond to reasons, learn from feedback, and adjust?
4. Evaluate responsibility using the functional criterion instead

## Example
"The agent only explores because its reward signal encourages exploration — it's not genuinely curious." Ultimate responsibility: humans are only curious because evolution wired them that way. The functional question is whether the agent's exploration is responsive and adaptive, not whether it chose to be curious from scratch.

## Anti-Pattern
Don't use this to deny all responsibility by saying "nothing is truly anyone's fault." The point is to redirect from impossible metaphysical standards to practical, functional ones.
