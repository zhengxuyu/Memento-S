# Who Is the Author of Spamlet?

If a spam filter accidentally produces Hamlet character by character, is it the author? No — authorship requires understanding, not just output. A process that generates something meaningful by accident hasn't created it in any meaningful sense. The relationship between process and product matters.

## When to Apply
- Evaluating whether an agent's success was intentional or accidental
- Distinguishing designed solutions from lucky outcomes
- Assessing whether a system truly "solved" a problem

## Procedure
1. Identify the successful output or behavior
2. Ask: did the process that produced it have any understanding of what it was producing?
3. Check if the success is reproducible and generalizable
4. Determine if the process could explain or modify its solution
5. Attribute authorship proportional to understanding, not just output quality

## Example
An agent reaches a high score by executing random actions that happen to work. It "produced" a winning strategy, but it isn't the "author" of one — it can't reproduce, explain, or adapt the strategy. True authorship requires the agent to have a model of why the actions work.

## Anti-Pattern
Don't demand full conscious understanding for authorship. There's a spectrum between random spam and Shakespeare — most real authorship falls somewhere in between.
