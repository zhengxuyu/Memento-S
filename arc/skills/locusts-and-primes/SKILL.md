# Do Locusts Understand Prime Numbers?

Periodical cicadas emerge on prime-number year cycles (13, 17 years), which minimizes overlap with predator cycles. This is a brilliant "strategy" — but the cicadas don't understand prime numbers. Natural selection found the pattern without comprehension. Competence without comprehension is ubiquitous.

## When to Apply
- A system produces sophisticated outputs and you're tempted to infer it "understands"
- You confuse functional success with genuine comprehension
- Evaluating whether an AI, organism, or algorithm truly "knows" what it's doing

## Procedure
1. Identify the competent behavior in question
2. Ask: could this competence exist without any comprehension of why it works?
3. Look for a mechanistic explanation that doesn't require understanding
4. If one exists, don't attribute comprehension without further evidence
5. Reserve "understanding" for cases where the system can flexibly explain and adapt its strategy

## Example
An agent consistently avoids certain grid cells and you conclude it "knows" they're dangerous. But the agent may simply have learned a stimulus-response pattern — avoid color X — without any model of danger. The competence (avoidance) doesn't require comprehension (a theory of harm).

## Anti-Pattern
Don't go too far the other way — denying comprehension wherever competence exists. The point is to not *assume* comprehension; some systems genuinely do understand.
